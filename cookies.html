Cookie Policy

Effective: May 23, 2026 · Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies we use on growthos-ai.com, what they do, and how you can control them. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser by sites you visit. They let sites remember your preferences, count visitors, and enable certain features. We also use related technologies like pixels, local storage, and SDKs — the term “cookies” in this policy covers all of them.

How we use cookies

Strictly necessary

These keep the site functioning — remembering form state during your strategy-call booking, holding your voice-agent session, and basic security. They can’t be turned off through this policy because the site won’t work without them.

Analytics

We use lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which content brings operators in and where they drop off. We do not use this data to build cross-site advertising profiles of you.

Marketing & conversion tracking

If you arrive from a paid ad campaign we may set a conversion pixel from Meta, Google, or another ad network so we can measure performance. These tools may set cookies that are subject to those vendors’ own policies.

Voice agent

The ElevenLabs voice agent embedded on this site uses cookies and local storage to maintain your conversation state during a session.

Managing cookies

You can clear and block cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers also offer a “Do Not Track” signal which we respect for analytics where technically feasible. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the site.

To opt out of cross-site interest-based advertising more broadly, see the NAI opt-out tool or the DAA opt-out tool.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at privacy@growthos-ai.com.

Note: If you serve users in the EU/UK/Brazil, you may need a consent banner before non-essential cookies fire. We’ll wire one in when the site is pointed at the production domain — just say the word.
