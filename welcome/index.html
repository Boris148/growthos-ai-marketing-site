Onboarding FAQ

How fast do we go live?

Most clients are live with AI Voice Caller and at least one ad campaign within 14 days of payment + intake completion. Full stack (lead gen + voice + database reactivation + CRM) typically by day 30.

What if I already have a CRM?

We integrate with anything — HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, custom. GoHighLevel is our default if you don’t have one or want to switch — we eat the build cost in that case.

Why no credit card option for the setup fee?

Card fees on this size payment would be over $1,000. We’d rather pass that savings to you in implementation time. Wire is free, instant, and bank-grade secure. ACH is the backup.

Can I see the contract before paying?

Yes — Step 1 is to sign. Payment is Step 2. Signed MSA must precede any work and any payment.

Who do I contact if something breaks?

Your dedicated Slack channel and a 24/7 monitored inbox at support@growthos-ai.com. AI Voice Caller has a 99.95% uptime SLA in your contract.
