How fast does this stand up?

Typical engagement is live in 14–21 days. The Voice Caller is calling new leads within the first 7. CRM build, pixel install, and intent audiences run in parallel during onboarding so nothing waits behind the slowest piece.

Is the AI voice caller going to feel robotic to my leads?

No. It’s built on ElevenLabs conversational AI, trained on your specific offer and objection handling, and tuned through dozens of real calls before it goes live. Most leads can’t tell. The ones who can, don’t care — they care that someone called back in under a minute. You can speak to ours right now via the widget on this page.

Do I have to rip out my current CRM?

No. We either run a full GoHighLevel build or we integrate cleanly with what you already use. We don’t force tooling — we force outcomes. If your existing stack works, we layer on top of it.

What data are you actually using? Is this compliant?

The Super Pixel runs on a proprietary identity-resolution data stack we partner with, fully sourced from compliant first- and second-party data networks. All outbound (voice, SMS, email) is TCPA- and DNC-compliant by default; consent and suppression are baked into the CRM build, not bolted on.

What won’t you do?

We won’t take on clients we can’t move the needle for. We don’t do ecommerce drop-shipping, MLM, or anything we’d be embarrassed to put our name on. And we don’t publish pricing — the strategy call is the qualifier on both sides.
